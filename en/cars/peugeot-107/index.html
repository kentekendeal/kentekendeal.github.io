We estimate a 2014 Peugeot 107 in the Netherlands at about €3,000 – €4,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €11,726.

How much does a Peugeot 107 cost per month?

For a 2014 Peugeot 107 we calculate about €148 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €16, fuel €117 and depreciation €15. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Peugeot 107?

At the APK inspection of the Peugeot 107, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (26% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (18%) and insufficient tyre tread (18%). 54% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 367 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

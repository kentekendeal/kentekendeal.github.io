Frequently asked questions

What is a 2019 Ford C-Max worth?

We estimate a 2019 Ford C-Max in the Netherlands at about €12,000 – €16,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €35,709.

How much does a Ford C-Max cost per month?

For a 2019 Ford C-Max we calculate about €263 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €66, fuel €138 and depreciation €59. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Ford C-Max?

At the APK inspection of the Ford C-Max, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (16% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (14%) and tyre pressure incorrect (12%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 819 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Ford C-Max cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 15,407 Ford C-Max cars built in 2012 or later.

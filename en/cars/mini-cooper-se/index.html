Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mini Cooper SE worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mini Cooper SE in the Netherlands at about €15,000 – €20,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €39,334.

How much does a Mini Cooper SE cost per month?

For a 2021 Mini Cooper SE we calculate about €226 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €36, electricity €51 and depreciation €139. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mini Cooper SE?

At the APK inspection of the Mini Cooper SE, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (12% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11%) and windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (10%). 75% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 204 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

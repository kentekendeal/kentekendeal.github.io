Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Opel Astra worth?

We estimate a 2021 Opel Astra in the Netherlands at about €10,250 – €14,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €29,462.

How much does a Opel Astra cost per month?

For a 2021 Opel Astra we calculate about €268 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €53, fuel €119 and depreciation €97. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Opel Astra?

At the APK inspection of the Opel Astra, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (12% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11%) and insufficient tyre tread (11%). 70% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 939 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Opel Astra cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 66,612 Opel Astra cars built in 2012 or later.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 BMW X1 worth?

We estimate a 2021 BMW X1 in the Netherlands at about €22,500 – €30,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €57,497.

How much does a BMW X1 cost per month?

For a 2021 BMW X1 we calculate about €437 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €89, fuel €138 and depreciation €209. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the BMW X1?

At the APK inspection of the BMW X1, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (12% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%) and insufficient tyre tread (10%). 77% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 922 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many BMW X1 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 35,366 BMW X1 cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a BMW X1 use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (98%), diesel (2%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

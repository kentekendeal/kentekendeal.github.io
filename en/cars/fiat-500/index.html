Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Fiat 500 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Fiat 500 in the Netherlands at about €8,750 – €11,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €24,737.

How much does a Fiat 500 cost per month?

For a 2021 Fiat 500 we calculate about €229 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €37, fuel €111 and depreciation €81. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Fiat 500?

At the APK inspection of the Fiat 500, inspectors most often found: dipped beam incorrectly aimed (12% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (11%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11%). 63% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 975 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

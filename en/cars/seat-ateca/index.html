Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Seat Ateca worth?

We estimate a 2021 Seat Ateca in the Netherlands at about €16,000 – €21,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €41,016.

How much does a Seat Ateca cost per month?

For a 2021 Seat Ateca we calculate about €341 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €59, fuel €133 and depreciation €149. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Seat Ateca?

At the APK inspection of the Seat Ateca, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (12% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (9%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (7%). 78% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 464 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Seat Ateca cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 15,804 Seat Ateca cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Seat Ateca use?

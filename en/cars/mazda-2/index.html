Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mazda 2 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mazda 2 in the Netherlands at about €9,250 – €12,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €21,972.

How much does a Mazda 2 cost per month?

For a 2021 Mazda 2 we calculate about €229 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €31, fuel €111 and depreciation €86. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mazda 2?

At the APK inspection of the Mazda 2, inspectors most often found: windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (11% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (11%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (10%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 975 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

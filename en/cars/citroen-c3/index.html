Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Citroën C3 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Citroën C3 in the Netherlands at about €7,750 – €10,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €22,302.

How much does a Citroën C3 cost per month?

For a 2021 Citroën C3 we calculate about €218 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €30, fuel €115 and depreciation €73. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Citroën C3?

At the APK inspection of the Citroën C3, inspectors most often found: number plate light not working (properly) / missing (18% of cars), tyre damaged (12%) and tyre pressure incorrect (11%). 60% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 931 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Citroën C3 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 51,389 Citroën C3 cars built in 2012 or later.

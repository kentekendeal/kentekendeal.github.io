Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Volkswagen Polo worth?

We estimate a 2021 Volkswagen Polo in the Netherlands at about €10,750 – €14,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €25,725.

How much does a Volkswagen Polo cost per month?

For a 2021 Volkswagen Polo we calculate about €250 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €38, fuel €111 and depreciation €101. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volkswagen Polo?

At the APK inspection of the Volkswagen Polo, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (15% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (14%) and tyre pressure incorrect (14%). 63% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 973 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Volkswagen Polo cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 189,172 Volkswagen Polo cars built in 2012 or later.

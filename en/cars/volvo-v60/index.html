Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Volvo V60 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Volvo V60 in the Netherlands at about €20,500 – €28,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €52,944.

How much does a Volvo V60 cost per month?

For a 2021 Volvo V60 we calculate about €445 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €104, fuel €148 and depreciation €193. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volvo V60?

At the APK inspection of the Volvo V60, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (14% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11%) and tyre pressure incorrect (8%). 68% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 947 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

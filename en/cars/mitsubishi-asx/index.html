Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mitsubishi ASX worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mitsubishi ASX in the Netherlands at about €13,500 – €18,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €34,524.

How much does a Mitsubishi ASX cost per month?

For a 2021 Mitsubishi ASX we calculate about €343 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €66, fuel €152 and depreciation €126. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mitsubishi ASX?

At the APK inspection of the Mitsubishi ASX, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (13% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%) and windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (11%). 68% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 926 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

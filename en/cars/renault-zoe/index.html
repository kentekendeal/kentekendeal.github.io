What is a 2021 Renault Zoe worth?

How much does a Renault Zoe cost per month?

For a 2021 Renault Zoe we calculate about €211 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €44, electricity €63 and depreciation €104. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Renault Zoe?

At the APK inspection of the Renault Zoe, inspectors most often found: too much play in the steering knuckle (ball joint) (18% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (14%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%). 53% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 763 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

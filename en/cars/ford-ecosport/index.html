Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Ford EcoSport worth?

We estimate a 2021 Ford EcoSport in the Netherlands at about €11,250 – €15,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €29,048.

How much does a Ford EcoSport cost per month?

For a 2021 Ford EcoSport we calculate about €288 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €55, fuel €127 and depreciation €106. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Ford EcoSport?

At the APK inspection of the Ford EcoSport, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (11% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (11%) and insufficient tyre tread (9%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 710 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Ford EcoSport cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 13,173 Ford EcoSport cars built in 2012 or later.

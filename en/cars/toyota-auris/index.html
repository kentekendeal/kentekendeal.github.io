Frequently asked questions

What is a 2019 Toyota Auris worth?

We estimate a 2019 Toyota Auris in the Netherlands at about €11,000 – €15,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €30,593.

How much does a Toyota Auris cost per month?

For a 2019 Toyota Auris we calculate about €212 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €57, fuel €101 and depreciation €54. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Toyota Auris?

At the APK inspection of the Toyota Auris, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (14% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (14%) and number plate light not working (properly) / missing (13%). 63% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 813 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Toyota Auris cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 41,001 Toyota Auris cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Toyota Auris use?

For recent years we mostly see: hybrid (84%), petrol (16%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

What is a 2019 Hyundai ix20 worth?

We estimate a 2019 Hyundai ix20 in the Netherlands at about €11,500 – €15,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €32,122.

How much does a Hyundai ix20 cost per month?

For a 2019 Hyundai ix20 we calculate about €268 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €51, fuel €160 and depreciation €57. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Hyundai ix20?

At the APK inspection of the Hyundai ix20, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (14% of cars), indicator colour incorrect / not allowed (14%) and side light not working (properly) (10%). 60% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 805 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

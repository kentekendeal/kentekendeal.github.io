Frequently asked questions

What is a 2019 Fiat Punto worth?

We estimate a 2019 Fiat Punto in the Netherlands at about €4,750 – €6,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €15,874.

How much does a Fiat Punto cost per month?

For a 2019 Fiat Punto we calculate about €164 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €37, fuel €103 and depreciation €23. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Fiat Punto?

At the APK inspection of the Fiat Punto, inspectors most often found: steering rack boot damaged, not sealing (23% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (17%) and tyre pressure incorrect (15%). 52% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 787 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Fiat Punto cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 10,413 Fiat Punto cars built in 2012 or later.

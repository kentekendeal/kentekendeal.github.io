Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Kia Picanto worth?

We estimate a 2021 Kia Picanto in the Netherlands at about €7,000 – €9,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €16,387.

How much does a Kia Picanto cost per month?

For a 2021 Kia Picanto we calculate about €189 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €23, fuel €101 and depreciation €64. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Kia Picanto?

At the APK inspection of the Kia Picanto, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (14% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (11%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (10%). 65% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 985 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

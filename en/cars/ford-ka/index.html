Frequently asked questions

What is a 2016 Ford Ka worth?

We estimate a 2016 Ford Ka in the Netherlands at about €3,500 – €4,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €12,450.

How much does a Ford Ka cost per month?

For a 2016 Ford Ka we calculate about €155 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €20, fuel €117 and depreciation €18. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Ford Ka?

At the APK inspection of the Ford Ka, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (17% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (17%) and exhaust system leaking / unsafe exhaust gas outlet (16%). 59% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 595 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

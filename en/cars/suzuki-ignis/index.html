Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Suzuki Ignis worth?

We estimate a 2021 Suzuki Ignis in the Netherlands at about €8,750 – €11,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €20,542.

How much does a Suzuki Ignis cost per month?

For a 2021 Suzuki Ignis we calculate about €212 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €22, fuel €109 and depreciation €81. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Suzuki Ignis?

At the APK inspection of the Suzuki Ignis, inspectors most often found: windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (13% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (11%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (8%). 75% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 463 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Suzuki Ignis cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 22,231 Suzuki Ignis cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Suzuki Ignis use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (53%), hybrid (47%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

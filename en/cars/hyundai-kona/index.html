Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Hyundai Kona worth?

We estimate a 2021 Hyundai Kona in the Netherlands at about €13,250 – €18,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €35,005.

How much does a Hyundai Kona cost per month?

For a 2021 Hyundai Kona we calculate about €220 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €42, electricity €54 and depreciation €124. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Hyundai Kona?

At the APK inspection of the Hyundai Kona, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (9%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (7%). 78% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 352 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Hyundai Kona cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 51,034 Hyundai Kona cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Hyundai Kona use?

For recent years we mostly see: electric (52%), hybrid (35%), petrol (13%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

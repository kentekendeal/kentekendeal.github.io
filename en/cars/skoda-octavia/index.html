Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Skoda Octavia worth?

We estimate a 2021 Skoda Octavia in the Netherlands at about €14,750 – €19,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €34,795.

How much does a Skoda Octavia cost per month?

For a 2021 Skoda Octavia we calculate about €317 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €61, fuel €119 and depreciation €137. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Skoda Octavia?

At the APK inspection of the Skoda Octavia, inspectors most often found: dipped beam incorrectly aimed (13% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%) and tyre pressure incorrect (12%). 69% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 934 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Skoda Octavia cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 61,711 Skoda Octavia cars built in 2012 or later.

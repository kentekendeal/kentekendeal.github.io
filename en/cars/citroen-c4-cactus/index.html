What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Citroën C4 Cactus?

At the APK inspection of the Citroën C4 Cactus, inspectors most often found: number plate light not working (properly) / missing (20% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%) and tyre pressure incorrect (12%). 61% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 671 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2020 Volvo V40 worth?

We estimate a 2020 Volvo V40 in the Netherlands at about €11,750 – €16,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €33,546.

How much does a Volvo V40 cost per month?

For a 2020 Volvo V40 we calculate about €257 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €63, fuel €136 and depreciation €58. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volvo V40?

At the APK inspection of the Volvo V40, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (14% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (14%) and tyre pressure incorrect (13%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 874 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Volvo V40 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 33,107 Volvo V40 cars built in 2012 or later.

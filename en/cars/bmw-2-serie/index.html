Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 BMW 2 Series worth?

We estimate a 2021 BMW 2 Series in the Netherlands at about €19,000 – €25,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €48,567.

How much does a BMW 2 Series cost per month?

For a 2021 BMW 2 Series we calculate about €378 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €69, fuel €133 and depreciation €177. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the BMW 2 Series?

At the APK inspection of the BMW 2 Series, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (12% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (7%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (7%). 77% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 687 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many BMW 2 Series cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 29,295 BMW 2 Series cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a BMW 2 Series use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (94%), plug-in hybrid (5%), diesel (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

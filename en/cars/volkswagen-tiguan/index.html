Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Volkswagen Tiguan worth?

We estimate a 2021 Volkswagen Tiguan in the Netherlands at about €21,250 – €28,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €50,390.

How much does a Volkswagen Tiguan cost per month?

For a 2021 Volkswagen Tiguan we calculate about €425 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €82, fuel €144 and depreciation €198. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volkswagen Tiguan?

At the APK inspection of the Volkswagen Tiguan, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (14% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (12%) and insufficient tyre tread (10%). 77% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 950 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Volkswagen Tiguan cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 82,530 Volkswagen Tiguan cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Volkswagen Tiguan use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (98%), diesel (2%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

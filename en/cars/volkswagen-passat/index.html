Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Volkswagen Passat worth?

We estimate a 2021 Volkswagen Passat in the Netherlands at about €21,250 – €28,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €50,583.

How much does a Volkswagen Passat cost per month?

For a 2021 Volkswagen Passat we calculate about €417 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €85, fuel €133 and depreciation €199. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volkswagen Passat?

At the APK inspection of the Volkswagen Passat, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (14% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (12%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%). 70% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 952 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Volkswagen Passat cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 42,753 Volkswagen Passat cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Volkswagen Passat use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (84%), diesel (16%), plug-in hybrid (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

How much does a Mazda CX-5 cost per month?

For a 2021 Mazda CX-5 we calculate about €402 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €76, fuel €150 and depreciation €177. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mazda CX-5?

At the APK inspection of the Mazda CX-5, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (13% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%) and too much play in the steering knuckle (ball joint) (8%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 983 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

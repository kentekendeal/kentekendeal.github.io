Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Skoda Citigo worth?

We estimate a 2021 Skoda Citigo in the Netherlands at about €10,000 – €13,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €23,798.

How much does a Skoda Citigo cost per month?

For a 2021 Skoda Citigo we calculate about €239 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €44, fuel €101 and depreciation €94. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Skoda Citigo?

At the APK inspection of the Skoda Citigo, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (16% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (13%) and insufficient tyre tread (11%). 72% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 934 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Skoda Citigo cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 20,165 Skoda Citigo cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Skoda Citigo use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (83%), electric (17%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Ford Kuga worth?

We estimate a 2021 Ford Kuga in the Netherlands at about €17,000 – €23,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €43,910.

How much does a Ford Kuga cost per month?

For a 2021 Ford Kuga we calculate about €410 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €96, fuel €154 and depreciation €160. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Ford Kuga?

At the APK inspection of the Ford Kuga, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (13% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (11%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (11%). 68% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 923 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Ford Kuga cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 58,448 Ford Kuga cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Ford Kuga use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (99%), lpg (1%), diesel (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

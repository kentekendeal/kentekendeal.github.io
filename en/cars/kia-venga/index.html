Frequently asked questions

What is a 2019 Kia Venga worth?

We estimate a 2019 Kia Venga in the Netherlands at about €10,500 – €14,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €29,339.

How much does a Kia Venga cost per month?

For a 2019 Kia Venga we calculate about €261 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €49, fuel €160 and depreciation €52. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Kia Venga?

At the APK inspection of the Kia Venga, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (14% of cars), side light not working (properly) (13%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (10%). 67% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 807 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Seat Leon worth?

We estimate a 2021 Seat Leon in the Netherlands at about €14,250 – €19,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €36,469.

How much does a Seat Leon cost per month?

For a 2021 Seat Leon we calculate about €313 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €64, fuel €117 and depreciation €133. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Seat Leon?

At the APK inspection of the Seat Leon, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (16% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (15%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%). 69% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 935 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Seat Leon cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 39,829 Seat Leon cars built in 2012 or later.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Volvo XC90 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Volvo XC90 in the Netherlands at about €36,750 – €49,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €94,077.

How much does a Volvo XC90 cost per month?

For a 2021 Volvo XC90 we calculate about €574 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €144, fuel €88 and depreciation €343. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volvo XC90?

At the APK inspection of the Volvo XC90, inspectors most often found: steering or ball joint wear of 1.0 mm or less (18% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (16%) and insufficient tyre tread (13%). 67% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 864 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Volvo XC90 cars are there in the Netherlands?

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Suzuki Vitara worth?

We estimate a 2021 Suzuki Vitara in the Netherlands at about €11,750 – €16,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €28,032.

How much does a Suzuki Vitara cost per month?

For a 2021 Suzuki Vitara we calculate about €283 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €46, fuel €127 and depreciation €110. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Suzuki Vitara?

At the APK inspection of the Suzuki Vitara, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11% of cars), windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (10%) and tyre pressure incorrect (10%). 73% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 610 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Suzuki Vitara cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 29,731 Suzuki Vitara cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Suzuki Vitara use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (99%), lpg (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Opel Crossland worth?

We estimate a 2021 Opel Crossland in the Netherlands at about €10,500 – €14,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €29,810.

How much does a Opel Crossland cost per month?

For a 2021 Opel Crossland we calculate about €266 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €45, fuel €123 and depreciation €98. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Opel Crossland?

At the APK inspection of the Opel Crossland, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (9% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (8%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (7%). 75% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 362 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Opel Crossland cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 29,385 Opel Crossland cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Opel Crossland use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (99%), diesel (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Toyota Corolla worth?

We estimate a 2021 Toyota Corolla in the Netherlands at about €14,000 – €19,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €33,193.

How much does a Toyota Corolla cost per month?

For a 2021 Toyota Corolla we calculate about €288 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €62, fuel €96 and depreciation €131. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Toyota Corolla?

At the APK inspection of the Toyota Corolla, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (10% of cars), windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (8%) and insufficient tyre tread (6%). 81% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 221 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Toyota Corolla cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 44,114 Toyota Corolla cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Toyota Corolla use?

For recent years we mostly see: hybrid (97%), petrol (3%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2020 Suzuki Celerio worth?

We estimate a 2020 Suzuki Celerio in the Netherlands at about €4,750 – €6,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €12,567.

How much does a Suzuki Celerio cost per month?

For a 2020 Suzuki Celerio we calculate about €134 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €17, fuel €94 and depreciation €23. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Suzuki Celerio?

At the APK inspection of the Suzuki Celerio, inspectors most often found: windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (22% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (18%) and insufficient tyre tread (11%). 63% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 618 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Suzuki Celerio cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 12,867 Suzuki Celerio cars built in 2012 or later.

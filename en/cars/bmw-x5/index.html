Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 BMW X5 worth?

We estimate a 2021 BMW X5 in the Netherlands at about €42,750 – €57,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €109,844.

How much does a BMW X5 cost per month?

For a 2021 BMW X5 we calculate about €774 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €160, fuel €214 and depreciation €400. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the BMW X5?

At the APK inspection of the BMW X5, inspectors most often found: steering or ball joint wear of 1.0 mm or less (16% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (10%) and tyre pressure incorrect (10%). 78% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 848 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many BMW X5 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 26,195 BMW X5 cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a BMW X5 use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (59%), diesel (26%), plug-in hybrid (15%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

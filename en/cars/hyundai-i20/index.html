Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Hyundai i20 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Hyundai i20 in the Netherlands at about €9,500 – €13,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €22,724.

How much does a Hyundai i20 cost per month?

For a 2021 Hyundai i20 we calculate about €239 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €36, fuel €113 and depreciation €89. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Hyundai i20?

At the APK inspection of the Hyundai i20, inspectors most often found: number plate light not working (properly) / missing (12% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (10%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (10%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 965 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Toyota RAV4 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Toyota RAV4 in the Netherlands at about €22,000 – €29,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €52,069.

How much does a Toyota RAV4 cost per month?

For a 2021 Toyota RAV4 we calculate about €407 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €91, fuel €111 and depreciation €205. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Toyota RAV4?

At the APK inspection of the Toyota RAV4, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (10% of cars), windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (9%) and number plate light not working (properly) / missing (7%). 76% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 955 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Toyota RAV4 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 33,537 Toyota RAV4 cars built in 2012 or later.

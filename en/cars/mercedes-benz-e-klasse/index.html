Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mercedes-Benz E-Class worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mercedes-Benz E-Class in the Netherlands at about €30,000 – €40,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €77,593.

How much does a Mercedes-Benz E-Class cost per month?

For a 2021 Mercedes-Benz E-Class we calculate about €645 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €204, fuel €107 and depreciation €334. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mercedes-Benz E-Class?

At the APK inspection of the Mercedes-Benz E-Class, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (18% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (14%) and tyre pressure incorrect (11%). 75% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 985 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Mercedes-Benz E-Class cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 22,708 Mercedes-Benz E-Class cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Mercedes-Benz E-Class use?

For recent years we mostly see: diesel (73%), petrol (10%), hybrid (10%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

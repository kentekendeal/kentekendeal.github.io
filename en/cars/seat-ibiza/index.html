Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Seat Ibiza worth?

We estimate a 2021 Seat Ibiza in the Netherlands at about €9,000 – €12,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €23,377.

How much does a Seat Ibiza cost per month?

For a 2021 Seat Ibiza we calculate about €226 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €36, fuel €105 and depreciation €85. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Seat Ibiza?

At the APK inspection of the Seat Ibiza, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (15% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (15%) and insufficient tyre tread (15%). 61% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 924 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Kia Niro worth?

We estimate a 2021 Kia Niro in the Netherlands at about €16,000 – €21,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €38,162.

How much does a Kia Niro cost per month?

For a 2021 Kia Niro we calculate about €328 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €82, fuel €96 and depreciation €150. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Kia Niro?

At the APK inspection of the Kia Niro, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (16% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (9%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%). 72% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 468 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Kia Niro cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 88,644 Kia Niro cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Kia Niro use?

For recent years we mostly see: hybrid (86%), electric (14%), plug-in hybrid (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

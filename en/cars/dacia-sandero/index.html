Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Dacia Sandero worth?

We estimate a 2021 Dacia Sandero in the Netherlands at about €7,000 – €9,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €18,094.

How much does a Dacia Sandero cost per month?

For a 2021 Dacia Sandero we calculate about €232 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €39, fuel €127 and depreciation €66. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Dacia Sandero?

At the APK inspection of the Dacia Sandero, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (17% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (14%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (14%). 61% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 1,072 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

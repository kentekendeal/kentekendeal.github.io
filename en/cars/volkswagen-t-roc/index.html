Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Volkswagen T-Roc worth?

We estimate a 2021 Volkswagen T-Roc in the Netherlands at about €16,500 – €22,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €39,158.

How much does a Volkswagen T-Roc cost per month?

For a 2021 Volkswagen T-Roc we calculate about €340 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €55, fuel €131 and depreciation €154. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volkswagen T-Roc?

At the APK inspection of the Volkswagen T-Roc, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (10% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (9%) and insufficient tyre tread (5%). 84% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 314 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Volkswagen T-Roc cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 57,950 Volkswagen T-Roc cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Volkswagen T-Roc use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (98%), diesel (2%), lpg (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

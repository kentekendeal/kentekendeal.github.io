Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 BMW 5 Series worth?

We estimate a 2021 BMW 5 Series in the Netherlands at about €33,250 – €45,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €85,360.

How much does a BMW 5 Series cost per month?

For a 2021 BMW 5 Series we calculate about €567 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €106, fuel €150 and depreciation €311. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the BMW 5 Series?

At the APK inspection of the BMW 5 Series, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (15% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (10%) and tyre pressure incorrect (8%). 76% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 889 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many BMW 5 Series cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 40,588 BMW 5 Series cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a BMW 5 Series use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (82%), diesel (9%), plug-in hybrid (9%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2015 Suzuki Alto worth?

We estimate a 2015 Suzuki Alto in the Netherlands at about €3,000 – €4,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €10,049.

How much does a Suzuki Alto cost per month?

For a 2015 Suzuki Alto we calculate about €151 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €20, fuel €117 and depreciation €15. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Suzuki Alto?

At the APK inspection of the Suzuki Alto, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (23% of cars), side light not working (properly) (23%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (21%). 53% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 494 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

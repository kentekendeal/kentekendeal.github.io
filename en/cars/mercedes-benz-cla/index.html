Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mercedes-Benz CLA worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mercedes-Benz CLA in the Netherlands at about €19,750 – €26,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €50,678.

How much does a Mercedes-Benz CLA cost per month?

For a 2021 Mercedes-Benz CLA we calculate about €381 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €74, fuel €123 and depreciation €185. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mercedes-Benz CLA?

At the APK inspection of the Mercedes-Benz CLA, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (14% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (13%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (10%). 75% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 795 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Mercedes-Benz CLA cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 31,157 Mercedes-Benz CLA cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Mercedes-Benz CLA use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (93%), diesel (7%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

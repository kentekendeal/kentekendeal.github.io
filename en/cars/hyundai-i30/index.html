What is a 2021 Hyundai i30 worth?

How much does a Hyundai i30 cost per month?

For a 2021 Hyundai i30 we calculate about €294 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €55, fuel €115 and depreciation €124. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Hyundai i30?

At the APK inspection of the Hyundai i30, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (15% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (14%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (13%). 62% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 948 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

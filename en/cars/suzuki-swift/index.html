Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Suzuki Swift worth?

We estimate a 2021 Suzuki Swift in the Netherlands at about €8,500 – €11,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €20,431.

How much does a Suzuki Swift cost per month?

For a 2021 Suzuki Swift we calculate about €203 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €23, fuel €99 and depreciation €80. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Suzuki Swift?

At the APK inspection of the Suzuki Swift, inspectors most often found: windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (16% of cars), number plate light not working (properly) / missing (12%) and tyre pressure incorrect (11%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 942 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

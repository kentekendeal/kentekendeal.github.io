Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mercedes-Benz B-Class worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mercedes-Benz B-Class in the Netherlands at about €18,500 – €25,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €47,265.

How much does a Mercedes-Benz B-Class cost per month?

For a 2021 Mercedes-Benz B-Class we calculate about €372 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €77, fuel €123 and depreciation €172. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mercedes-Benz B-Class?

At the APK inspection of the Mercedes-Benz B-Class, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (14% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (10%) and insufficient tyre tread (10%). 74% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 980 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Mercedes-Benz B-Class cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 30,322 Mercedes-Benz B-Class cars built in 2012 or later.

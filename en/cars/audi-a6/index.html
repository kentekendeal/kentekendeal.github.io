Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Audi A6 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Audi A6 in the Netherlands at about €35,500 – €48,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €90,880.

How much does a Audi A6 cost per month?

For a 2021 Audi A6 we calculate about €597 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €122, fuel €144 and depreciation €331. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Audi A6?

At the APK inspection of the Audi A6, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (16% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (13%) and tyre pressure incorrect (13%). 75% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 943 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Audi A6 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 25,444 Audi A6 cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Audi A6 use?

For recent years we mostly see: hybrid (91%), petrol (6%), diesel (4%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Audi A3 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Audi A3 in the Netherlands at about €17,250 – €23,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €44,051.

How much does a Audi A3 cost per month?

For a 2021 Audi A3 we calculate about €352 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €66, fuel €125 and depreciation €160. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Audi A3?

At the APK inspection of the Audi A3, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (13% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (13%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%). 73% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 916 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Audi A3 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 75,406 Audi A3 cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Audi A3 use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (96%), diesel (3%), plug-in hybrid (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

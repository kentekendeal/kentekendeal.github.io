Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mercedes-Benz A-Class worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mercedes-Benz A-Class in the Netherlands at about €17,500 – €23,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €45,249.

How much does a Mercedes-Benz A-Class cost per month?

For a 2021 Mercedes-Benz A-Class we calculate about €358 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €70, fuel €123 and depreciation €165. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mercedes-Benz A-Class?

At the APK inspection of the Mercedes-Benz A-Class, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (17% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (14%) and wear on mechanical parts of the brake system (11%). 74% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 962 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Mercedes-Benz A-Class cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 50,649 Mercedes-Benz A-Class cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Mercedes-Benz A-Class use?

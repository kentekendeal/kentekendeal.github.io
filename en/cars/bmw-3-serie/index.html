Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 BMW 3 Series worth?

We estimate a 2021 BMW 3 Series in the Netherlands at about €25,000 – €33,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €64,022.

How much does a BMW 3 Series cost per month?

For a 2021 BMW 3 Series we calculate about €463 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €94, fuel €136 and depreciation €233. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the BMW 3 Series?

At the APK inspection of the BMW 3 Series, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (13% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (12%) and insufficient tyre tread (9%). 76% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 953 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many BMW 3 Series cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 65,114 BMW 3 Series cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a BMW 3 Series use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (91%), diesel (4%), plug-in hybrid (4%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

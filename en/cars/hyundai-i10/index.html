Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Hyundai i10 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Hyundai i10 in the Netherlands at about €7,250 – €9,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €16,988.

How much does a Hyundai i10 cost per month?

For a 2021 Hyundai i10 we calculate about €197 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €25, fuel €105 and depreciation €67. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Hyundai i10?

At the APK inspection of the Hyundai i10, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (17% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (16%) and tyre pressure incorrect (15%). 57% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 876 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

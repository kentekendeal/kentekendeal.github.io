What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Land Rover Range Rover Evoque?

At the APK inspection of the Land Rover Range Rover Evoque, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (13% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (9%). 73% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 937 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Renault Scenic worth?

We estimate a 2021 Renault Scenic in the Netherlands at about €13,250 – €18,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €37,974.

How much does a Renault Scenic cost per month?

For a 2021 Renault Scenic we calculate about €333 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €74, fuel €135 and depreciation €124. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Renault Scenic?

At the APK inspection of the Renault Scenic, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (10% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (10%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (8%). 70% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 452 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Renault Scenic cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 12,291 Renault Scenic cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Renault Scenic use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (99%), diesel (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

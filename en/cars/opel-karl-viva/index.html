What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Opel Karl / Viva?

At the APK inspection of the Opel Karl / Viva, inspectors most often found: indicator colour incorrect / not allowed (19% of cars), tyre pressure monitoring warning light shows a fault (16%) and insufficient tyre tread (15%). 63% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 452 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

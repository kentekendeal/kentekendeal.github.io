Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Peugeot 208 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Peugeot 208 in the Netherlands at about €10,750 – €14,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €27,760.

How much does a Peugeot 208 cost per month?

For a 2021 Peugeot 208 we calculate about €260 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €48, fuel €111 and depreciation €101. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Peugeot 208?

At the APK inspection of the Peugeot 208, inspectors most often found: number plate light not working (properly) / missing (19% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (15%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (12%). 60% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 946 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

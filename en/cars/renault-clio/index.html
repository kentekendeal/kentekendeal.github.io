Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Renault Clio worth?

We estimate a 2021 Renault Clio in the Netherlands at about €8,000 – €10,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €22,592.

How much does a Renault Clio cost per month?

For a 2021 Renault Clio we calculate about €213 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €37, fuel €101 and depreciation €74. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Renault Clio?

At the APK inspection of the Renault Clio, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (15% of cars), number plate light not working (properly) / missing (13%) and tyre pressure incorrect (11%). 58% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 985 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Nissan Micra worth?

We estimate a 2021 Nissan Micra in the Netherlands at about €8,250 – €11,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €23,544.

How much does a Nissan Micra cost per month?

For a 2021 Nissan Micra we calculate about €222 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €34, fuel €111 and depreciation €77. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Nissan Micra?

At the APK inspection of the Nissan Micra, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (14% of cars), windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (13%) and tyre pressure incorrect (12%). 61% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 991 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

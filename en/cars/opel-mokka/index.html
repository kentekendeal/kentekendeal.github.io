What is a 2021 Opel Mokka worth?

How much does a Opel Mokka cost per month?

For a 2021 Opel Mokka we calculate about €283 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €58, fuel €111 and depreciation €114. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Opel Mokka?

At the APK inspection of the Opel Mokka, inspectors most often found: ball joint boot damaged and not sealing (14% of cars), excessive leakage of other fluids (10%) and tyre pressure incorrect (9%). 67% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 833 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

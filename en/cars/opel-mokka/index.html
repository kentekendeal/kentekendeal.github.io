What is a 2021 Opel Mokka worth?

We estimate a 2021 Opel Mokka in the Netherlands at about €12,250 – €16,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €34,888.

How much does a Opel Mokka cost per month?

For a 2021 Opel Mokka we calculate about €314 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €58, fuel €142 and depreciation €114. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Opel Mokka?

At the APK inspection of the Opel Mokka, inspectors most often found: ball joint boot damaged and not sealing (15% of cars), excessive leakage of other fluids (10%) and tyre pressure incorrect (10%). 68% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 889 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

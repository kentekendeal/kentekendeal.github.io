Frequently asked questions

What is a 2018 Ford B-Max worth?

We estimate a 2018 Ford B-Max in the Netherlands at about €7,750 – €10,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €24,523.

How much does a Ford B-Max cost per month?

For a 2018 Ford B-Max we calculate about €203 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €48, fuel €117 and depreciation €38. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Ford B-Max?

At the APK inspection of the Ford B-Max, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (17% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (13%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (12%). 65% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 767 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

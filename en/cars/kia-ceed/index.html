What is a 2021 Kia Ceed worth?

We estimate a 2021 Kia Ceed in the Netherlands at about €12,750 – €17,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €30,269.

How much does a Kia Ceed cost per month?

For a 2021 Kia Ceed we calculate about €291 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €60, fuel €111 and depreciation €119. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Kia Ceed?

At the APK inspection of the Kia Ceed, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (13% of cars), number plate light not working (properly) / missing (13%) and insufficient tyre tread (13%). 65% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 990 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

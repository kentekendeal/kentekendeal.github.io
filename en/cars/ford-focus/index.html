Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Ford Focus worth?

We estimate a 2021 Ford Focus in the Netherlands at about €13,000 – €17,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €33,242.

How much does a Ford Focus cost per month?

For a 2021 Ford Focus we calculate about €295 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €58, fuel €115 and depreciation €121. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Ford Focus?

At the APK inspection of the Ford Focus, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (13% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (12%) and tyre pressure incorrect (12%). 64% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 986 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Ford Focus cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 100,376 Ford Focus cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Ford Focus use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (91%), diesel (9%), lpg (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Seat Arona worth?

We estimate a 2021 Seat Arona in the Netherlands at about €10,750 – €14,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €27,871.

How much does a Seat Arona cost per month?

For a 2021 Seat Arona we calculate about €261 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €40, fuel €119 and depreciation €101. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Seat Arona?

At the APK inspection of the Seat Arona, inspectors most often found: steering rack boot damaged, not sealing (13% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (12%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (8%). 73% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 367 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Seat Arona cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 17,852 Seat Arona cars built in 2012 or later.

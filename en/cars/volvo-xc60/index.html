Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Volvo XC60 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Volvo XC60 in the Netherlands at about €27,750 – €37,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €70,967.

How much does a Volvo XC60 cost per month?

For a 2021 Volvo XC60 we calculate about €545 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €123, fuel €164 and depreciation €258. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volvo XC60?

At the APK inspection of the Volvo XC60, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (14% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11%) and tyre pressure incorrect (10%). 72% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 1,006 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

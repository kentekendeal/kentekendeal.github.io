We estimate a 2021 Citroën C5 Aircross in the Netherlands at about €15,250 – €20,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €43,388.

How much does a Citroën C5 Aircross cost per month?

For a 2021 Citroën C5 Aircross we calculate about €363 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €86, fuel €135 and depreciation €142. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Citroën C5 Aircross?

At the APK inspection of the Citroën C5 Aircross, inspectors most often found: number plate light not working (properly) / missing (8% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (8%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (6%). 75% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 202 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

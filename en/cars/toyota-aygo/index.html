Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Toyota Aygo worth?

We estimate a 2021 Toyota Aygo in the Netherlands at about €6,500 – €8,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €15,247.

How much does a Toyota Aygo cost per month?

For a 2021 Toyota Aygo we calculate about €175 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €19, fuel €96 and depreciation €60. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Toyota Aygo?

At the APK inspection of the Toyota Aygo, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (14% of cars), windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (13%) and insufficient tyre tread (13%). 61% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 968 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Toyota Aygo cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 80,171 Toyota Aygo cars built in 2012 or later.

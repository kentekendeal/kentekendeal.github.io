Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mini Cooper worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mini Cooper in the Netherlands at about €15,500 – €21,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €37,076.

How much does a Mini Cooper cost per month?

For a 2021 Mini Cooper we calculate about €314 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €48, fuel €121 and depreciation €146. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mini Cooper?

At the APK inspection of the Mini Cooper, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (15% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (10%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%). 71% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 958 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Mini Cooper cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 26,826 Mini Cooper cars built in 2012 or later.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Hyundai Tucson worth?

We estimate a 2021 Hyundai Tucson in the Netherlands at about €18,500 – €25,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €43,846.

How much does a Hyundai Tucson cost per month?

For a 2021 Hyundai Tucson we calculate about €412 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €88, fuel €152 and depreciation €172. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Hyundai Tucson?

At the APK inspection of the Hyundai Tucson, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (11% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%) and insufficient tyre tread (8%). 73% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 664 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Hyundai Tucson cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 34,047 Hyundai Tucson cars built in 2012 or later.

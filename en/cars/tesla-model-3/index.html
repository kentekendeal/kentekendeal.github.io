Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Tesla Model 3 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Tesla Model 3 in the Netherlands at about €19,500 – €26,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €55,603.

How much does a Tesla Model 3 cost per month?

For a 2021 Tesla Model 3 we calculate about €301 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €63, electricity €56 and depreciation €182. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Tesla Model 3?

At the APK inspection of the Tesla Model 3, inspectors most often found: dipped beam incorrectly aimed (15% of cars), steering or ball joint wear of 1.0 mm or less (9%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%). 69% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 170 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

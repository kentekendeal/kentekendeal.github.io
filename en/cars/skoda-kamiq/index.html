What is a 2021 Skoda Kamiq worth?

How much does a Skoda Kamiq cost per month?

For a 2021 Skoda Kamiq we calculate about €280 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €45, fuel €119 and depreciation €116. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Skoda Kamiq?

At the APK inspection of the Skoda Kamiq, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (13% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (7%) and steering rack boot damaged, not sealing (5%). 74% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 188 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

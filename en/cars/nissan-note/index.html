Frequently asked questions

What is a 2017 Nissan Note worth?

We estimate a 2017 Nissan Note in the Netherlands at about €5,250 – €7,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €19,365.

How much does a Nissan Note cost per month?

For a 2017 Nissan Note we calculate about €174 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €31, fuel €117 and depreciation €26. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Nissan Note?

At the APK inspection of the Nissan Note, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (19% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (14%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (13%). 62% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 698 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

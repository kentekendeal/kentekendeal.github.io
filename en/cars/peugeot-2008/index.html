What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Peugeot 2008?

At the APK inspection of the Peugeot 2008, inspectors most often found: number plate light not working (properly) / missing (19% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (15%) and tyre pressure incorrect (13%). 62% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 843 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

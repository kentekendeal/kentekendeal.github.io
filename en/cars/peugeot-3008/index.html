How much does a Peugeot 3008 cost per month?

For a 2021 Peugeot 3008 we calculate about €369 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €78, fuel €129 and depreciation €162. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Peugeot 3008?

At the APK inspection of the Peugeot 3008, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (15% of cars), number plate light not working (properly) / missing (14%) and tyre pressure incorrect (14%). 63% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 875 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

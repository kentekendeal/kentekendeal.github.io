Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Nissan Juke worth?

We estimate a 2021 Nissan Juke in the Netherlands at about €11,000 – €15,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €31,683.

How much does a Nissan Juke cost per month?

For a 2021 Nissan Juke we calculate about €266 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €47, fuel €115 and depreciation €104. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Nissan Juke?

At the APK inspection of the Nissan Juke, inspectors most often found: windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (12% of cars), too much play in the steering knuckle (ball joint) (11%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11%). 63% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 951 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Nissan Juke cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 19,294 Nissan Juke cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Nissan Juke use?

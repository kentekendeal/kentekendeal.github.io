What is a 2021 Renault Captur worth?

We estimate a 2021 Renault Captur in the Netherlands at about €10,500 – €14,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €29,943.

How much does a Renault Captur cost per month?

For a 2021 Renault Captur we calculate about €280 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €55, fuel €127 and depreciation €98. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Renault Captur?

At the APK inspection of the Renault Captur, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (13% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11%) and insufficient tyre tread (11%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 865 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Toyota C-HR worth?

We estimate a 2021 Toyota C-HR in the Netherlands at about €15,250 – €20,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €36,534.

How much does a Toyota C-HR cost per month?

For a 2021 Toyota C-HR we calculate about €309 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €70, fuel €96 and depreciation €144. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Toyota C-HR?

At the APK inspection of the Toyota C-HR, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (11% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%) and windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (9%). 75% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 489 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Toyota C-HR cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 45,634 Toyota C-HR cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Toyota C-HR use?

For recent years we mostly see: hybrid (98%), plug-in hybrid (2%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

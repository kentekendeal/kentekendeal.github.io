Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Skoda Kodiaq worth?

We estimate a 2021 Skoda Kodiaq in the Netherlands at about €18,750 – €25,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €44,519.

How much does a Skoda Kodiaq cost per month?

For a 2021 Skoda Kodiaq we calculate about €402 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €77, fuel €150 and depreciation €175. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Skoda Kodiaq?

At the APK inspection of the Skoda Kodiaq, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (12% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (6%). 78% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 362 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Skoda Kodiaq cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 32,476 Skoda Kodiaq cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Skoda Kodiaq use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (95%), diesel (5%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

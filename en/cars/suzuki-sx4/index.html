What is a 2021 Suzuki SX4 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Suzuki SX4 in the Netherlands at about €12,000 – €16,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €28,420.

How much does a Suzuki SX4 cost per month?

For a 2021 Suzuki SX4 we calculate about €286 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €48, fuel €127 and depreciation €112. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Suzuki SX4?

At the APK inspection of the Suzuki SX4, inspectors most often found: windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (14% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (10%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (10%). 69% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 969 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

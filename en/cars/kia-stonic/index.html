Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Kia Stonic worth?

We estimate a 2021 Kia Stonic in the Netherlands at about €10,500 – €14,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €25,076.

How much does a Kia Stonic cost per month?

For a 2021 Kia Stonic we calculate about €260 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €43, fuel €119 and depreciation €99. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Kia Stonic?

At the APK inspection of the Kia Stonic, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (8%) and windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (6%). 81% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 366 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

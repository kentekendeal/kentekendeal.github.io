Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Audi Q2 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Audi Q2 in the Netherlands at about €17,250 – €23,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €44,424.

How much does a Audi Q2 cost per month?

For a 2021 Audi Q2 we calculate about €344 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €56, fuel €127 and depreciation €162. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Audi Q2?

At the APK inspection of the Audi Q2, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (13% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (6%). 79% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 497 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Audi Q2 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 18,692 Audi Q2 cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Audi Q2 use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (96%), diesel (3%), lpg (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

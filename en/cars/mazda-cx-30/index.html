What is a 2021 Mazda CX-30 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mazda CX-30 in the Netherlands at about €15,750 – €21,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €37,407.

How much does a Mazda CX-30 cost per month?

For a 2021 Mazda CX-30 we calculate about €335 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €67, fuel €121 and depreciation €147. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mazda CX-30?

At the APK inspection of the Mazda CX-30, inspectors most often found: windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (6% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (6%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (5%). 85% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 207 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Skoda Fabia worth?

We estimate a 2021 Skoda Fabia in the Netherlands at about €9,250 – €12,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €21,950.

How much does a Skoda Fabia cost per month?

For a 2021 Skoda Fabia we calculate about €228 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €35, fuel €107 and depreciation €86. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Skoda Fabia?

At the APK inspection of the Skoda Fabia, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (15% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (13%) and insufficient tyre tread (12%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 972 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

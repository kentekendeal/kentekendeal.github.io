For a 2017 Volvo V70 we calculate about €270 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €84, fuel €117 and depreciation €69. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volvo V70?

At the APK inspection of the Volvo V70, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (18% of cars), wear on mechanical parts of the brake system (12%) and tyre pressure incorrect (12%). 62% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 684 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

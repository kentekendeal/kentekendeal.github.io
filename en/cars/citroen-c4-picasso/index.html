What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Citroën C4 Picasso?

At the APK inspection of the Citroën C4 Picasso, inspectors most often found: number plate light not working (properly) / missing (23% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (18%) and insufficient tyre tread (16%). 60% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 806 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

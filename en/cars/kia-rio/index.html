Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Kia Rio worth?

We estimate a 2021 Kia Rio in the Netherlands at about €9,750 – €13,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €23,480.

How much does a Kia Rio cost per month?

For a 2021 Kia Rio we calculate about €245 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €40, fuel €113 and depreciation €92. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Kia Rio?

At the APK inspection of the Kia Rio, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (16% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (16%) and insufficient tyre tread (14%). 63% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 952 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

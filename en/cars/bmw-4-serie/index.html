Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 BMW 4 Series worth?

We estimate a 2021 BMW 4 Series in the Netherlands at about €30,000 – €40,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €77,082.

How much does a BMW 4 Series cost per month?

For a 2021 BMW 4 Series we calculate about €516 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €85, fuel €150 and depreciation €281. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the BMW 4 Series?

At the APK inspection of the BMW 4 Series, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (10% of cars), dipped beam incorrectly aimed (10%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (9%). 79% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 784 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many BMW 4 Series cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 14,286 BMW 4 Series cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a BMW 4 Series use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (99%), diesel (1%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

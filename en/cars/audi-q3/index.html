Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Audi Q3 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Audi Q3 in the Netherlands at about €23,250 – €31,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €59,830.

How much does a Audi Q3 cost per month?

For a 2021 Audi Q3 we calculate about €451 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €91, fuel €142 and depreciation €218. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Audi Q3?

At the APK inspection of the Audi Q3, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (12% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (11%) and tyre pressure incorrect (11%). 74% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 947 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Audi Q3 cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 31,779 Audi Q3 cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Audi Q3 use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (76%), hybrid (21%), diesel (2%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Opel Grandland worth?

We estimate a 2021 Opel Grandland in the Netherlands at about €14,750 – €20,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €42,019.

How much does a Opel Grandland cost per month?

For a 2021 Opel Grandland we calculate about €344 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €76, fuel €131 and depreciation €138. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Opel Grandland?

At the APK inspection of the Opel Grandland, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (14% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (11%) and tyre pressure incorrect (10%). 73% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 375 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Opel Grandland cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 25,096 Opel Grandland cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Opel Grandland use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (95%), diesel (5%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

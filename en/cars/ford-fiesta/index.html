Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Ford Fiesta worth?

We estimate a 2021 Ford Fiesta in the Netherlands at about €9,000 – €12,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €23,123.

How much does a Ford Fiesta cost per month?

For a 2021 Ford Fiesta we calculate about €233 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €39, fuel €109 and depreciation €84. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Ford Fiesta?

At the APK inspection of the Ford Fiesta, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (15% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (14%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (13%). 59% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 964 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Volkswagen Up! worth?

We estimate a 2021 Volkswagen Up! in the Netherlands at about €8,500 – €11,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €20,071.

How much does a Volkswagen Up! cost per month?

For a 2021 Volkswagen Up! we calculate about €212 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €29, fuel €103 and depreciation €79. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Volkswagen Up!?

At the APK inspection of the Volkswagen Up!, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (17% of cars), tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (14%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (11%). 66% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 983 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

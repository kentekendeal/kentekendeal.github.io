Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mercedes-Benz GLA worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mercedes-Benz GLA in the Netherlands at about €22,500 – €30,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €57,782.

How much does a Mercedes-Benz GLA cost per month?

For a 2021 Mercedes-Benz GLA we calculate about €438 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €82, fuel €146 and depreciation €210. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mercedes-Benz GLA?

At the APK inspection of the Mercedes-Benz GLA, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (11% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (10%) and insufficient tyre tread (8%). 82% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 715 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Mercedes-Benz GLA cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 17,865 Mercedes-Benz GLA cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Mercedes-Benz GLA use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (98%), diesel (2%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

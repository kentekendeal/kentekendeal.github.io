Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mazda CX-3 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mazda CX-3 in the Netherlands at about €12,750 – €17,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €30,502.

How much does a Mazda CX-3 cost per month?

For a 2021 Mazda CX-3 we calculate about €295 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €46, fuel €129 and depreciation €120. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mazda CX-3?

At the APK inspection of the Mazda CX-3, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (10% of cars), seatbelt retractor not working (9%) and insufficient tyre tread (8%). 71% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 621 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

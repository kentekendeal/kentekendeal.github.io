Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Renault Megane worth?

We estimate a 2021 Renault Megane in the Netherlands at about €11,750 – €15,750, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €33,191.

How much does a Renault Megane cost per month?

For a 2021 Renault Megane we calculate about €295 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €66, fuel €121 and depreciation €109. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Renault Megane?

At the APK inspection of the Renault Megane, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (16% of cars), number plate light not working (properly) / missing (15%) and tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (14%). 54% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 962 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

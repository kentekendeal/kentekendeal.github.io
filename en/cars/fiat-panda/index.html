Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Fiat Panda worth?

We estimate a 2021 Fiat Panda in the Netherlands at about €6,000 – €8,000, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €16,966.

How much does a Fiat Panda cost per month?

For a 2021 Fiat Panda we calculate about €180 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €29, fuel €96 and depreciation €56. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Fiat Panda?

At the APK inspection of the Fiat Panda, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (15% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (14%) and windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (11%). 60% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 977 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Fiat Panda cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 30,738 Fiat Panda cars built in 2012 or later.

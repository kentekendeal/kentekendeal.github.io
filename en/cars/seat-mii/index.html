Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Seat Mii worth?

We estimate a 2021 Seat Mii in the Netherlands at about €9,250 – €12,500, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €23,632.

How much does a Seat Mii cost per month?

For a 2021 Seat Mii we calculate about €231 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €43, fuel €101 and depreciation €86. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Seat Mii?

At the APK inspection of the Seat Mii, inspectors most often found: insufficient tyre tread (13% of cars), tyre pressure incorrect (12%) and dipped beam incorrectly aimed (10%). 70% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 899 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Seat Mii cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 20,612 Seat Mii cars built in 2012 or later.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Citroën C1 worth?

We estimate a 2021 Citroën C1 in the Netherlands at about €5,500 – €7,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €15,420.

How much does a Citroën C1 cost per month?

For a 2021 Citroën C1 we calculate about €165 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €19, fuel €96 and depreciation €51. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Citroën C1?

At the APK inspection of the Citroën C1, inspectors most often found: tyre pressure incorrect (16% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (13%) and windscreen wipers give insufficient visibility (12%). 62% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 67%). Based on 946 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

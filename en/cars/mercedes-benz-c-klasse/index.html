Frequently asked questions

What is a 2021 Mercedes-Benz C-Class worth?

We estimate a 2021 Mercedes-Benz C-Class in the Netherlands at about €22,500 – €30,250, with average mileage and in normal condition. The average new price for that year was €57,460.

How much does a Mercedes-Benz C-Class cost per month?

For a 2021 Mercedes-Benz C-Class we calculate about €436 per month at 12,000 km per year: Dutch road tax €84, fuel €142 and depreciation €209. Insurance and maintenance come on top.

What are common APK (MOT) defects of the Mercedes-Benz C-Class?

At the APK inspection of the Mercedes-Benz C-Class, inspectors most often found: tyre(s) with 1.6 to 2.5 mm of tread left (16% of cars), insufficient tyre tread (13%) and steering or ball joint wear of 1.0 mm or less (12%). 74% of inspections had no defects (average across all popular models: 68%). Based on 942 inspections since June 2022 (RDW).

How many Mercedes-Benz C-Class cars are there in the Netherlands?

The RDW register lists 46,835 Mercedes-Benz C-Class cars built in 2012 or later.

What fuel does a Mercedes-Benz C-Class use?

For recent years we mostly see: petrol (78%), hybrid (16%), diesel (7%). This is based on a sample from the RDW register.

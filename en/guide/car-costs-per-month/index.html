What does a car cost per month in the Netherlands?

The purchase price is only the start. These are the costs that come back every month, and how to calculate them for your car.

The 6 cost items

  1. Depreciation. Often the biggest item, especially for young cars. A new car loses a lot of value in the first year, after which it slows down gradually. You only notice it when you sell, but it is real money.
  2. Fuel or electricity. Depends on consumption, price and how much you drive. Use the WLTP figure as a minimum; in practice it's often a bit higher.
  3. Road tax (motorrijtuigenbelasting, MRB). Depends on the weight, the fuel and your province. Diesels are more expensive, electric cars pay 70% of the petrol rate in 2026. Cars aged 40 years or more are exempt.
  4. Insurance. Third party (WA), third party plus limited cover (WA+ or beperkt casco) or comprehensive (allrisk). Depends strongly on your age, no-claims years, where you live and the car itself. Get a few quotes.
  5. Maintenance and repairs. Small and big services, tyres, brakes and unexpected repairs. For older cars this becomes a bigger and bigger item.
  6. APK (the Dutch MOT). A petrol or electric car needs its first APK after 4 years, then after 6 and 8 years, and every year after that. Diesel and LPG: after 3 years and then every year.

Worked example

Tip: don't just compare the purchase price of two cars, compare the cost per month. A cheaper diesel can end up more expensive per month than a slightly pricier petrol or hybrid car, because of the higher road tax.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest cost of a car?

For young cars it's almost always depreciation. For older cars, fuel, maintenance and repairs weigh more.

How often does my car need an APK (MOT) in the Netherlands?

A petrol or electric car needs its first APK after 4 years, then after 6 and 8 years, and then every year. A diesel or LPG car needs its first APK after 3 years and then every year.

Does an electric car pay road tax in the Netherlands?

Yes. In 2026 an electric passenger car pays 70% of the rate of a comparable petrol car, with a weight correction for the battery.
